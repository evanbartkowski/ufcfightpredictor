Independent UFC predictions. Evidence you can inspect.

MMA Cipher, also called MMACipher, is an independent UFC prediction and MMA statistics project. It brings together announced fight cards, fighter records, published forecasts, and the evidence behind each estimate.

This site is not affiliated with UFC, UFCStats, ESPN, or a sportsbook.

What goes into a forecast

UFCStats supplies recorded fight outcomes and fighter profile snapshots. ESPN supplies announced schedules, card placement, and available sportsbook moneylines. Profiles are normalized before use, and predictions use only eligible pre-event information.

Forecasts are produced by MMA Cipher’s automated models. They are estimates, have not been separately calibrated, and do not guarantee an outcome.

A record that preserves the original pick

Original issued forecasts remain saved. The live prediction record grades confirmed main-card bouts only. Prelims and bouts without verified card placement are excluded; draws and no contests are unscored. Historical model test scores describe a separate chronological evaluation sample.

Sources, code, and corrections

Explore the underlying sources at UFCStats and ESPN MMA. The implementation is available in the MMA Cipher source repository.

To report a data or software problem, open an issue on GitHub with the matchup and the source you want checked.
