Market-information policy

The serving model is market-free: untimestamped archive moneylines cannot improve its backtest while being absent at prediction time. Eligible refreshed profiles are normalized and merged only before issue time and event start.

Paired market comparison: insufficient_data. Eligible timestamped bouts: 0 across 0 event dates. At least 100 timestamp-verified bouts across 10 event dates are required. Untimestamped CSV odds are excluded.

Data and evaluation limits

Chronological replay reduces outcome leakage. Historical source completeness and the timestamps of imported attributes are not independently audited. Missing inputs use baseline values.

Replay builds fighter histories before each event day, and a chronological holdout measures later outcomes. Missing measurements are imputed. Agreement between estimators is not a calibrated uncertainty interval.
